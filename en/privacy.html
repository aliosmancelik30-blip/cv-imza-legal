Privacy Policy

CV İmza · Last updated: 11 October 2026

In short

CV İmza doesn’t require an account. Your CVs, photos and answers are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers. No advertising, tracking or analytics tools are used.

What is stored on your device

Your name, contact details, experience, education, skills, answers to profession questions and the photo you choose are kept in the app’s own storage to prepare your CV. Deleting the app deletes this data too.

Photos and files

A photo is read only when you choose one, to add it to your CV. PDFs stay on your device until you choose to share them; sharing happens through the app you pick.

Purchases

Payment is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. Your card details never reach us. To remember Premium access on your device, the purchase record provided by the store is kept in the device’s secure storage.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Your rights

All data stays on your device: you can edit or delete CVs inside the app, or remove everything by uninstalling it. For requests under data protection laws, including the GDPR and Turkey’s Law No. 6698 (KVKK), use the support link on the store page.
