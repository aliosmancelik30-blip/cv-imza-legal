CV İmza · Frequently asked questions

How do I move Premium to a new phone?

Open CV İmza on a phone signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account, then tap “Restore purchases” on the Premium screen or in Settings. You won’t pay again.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time purchase that unlocks all 60 premium designs and any designs added later. There are no recurring fees.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from: reportaproblem.apple.com for the App Store, or Play Store → Payments & subscriptions for Google Play.

Where are my CVs stored?

Only on your phone. There’s no account, cloud or advertising. Deleting the app deletes your CVs too, so we recommend exporting important CVs as PDFs.

Can I write my CV in English?

Yes. The app follows your phone’s language, and each CV has its own language setting in the Design & PDF section, so section headings can be English or Turkish.

How do I share my PDF?

When your CV is finished, tap “Download PDF” and pick Mail, Messages, WhatsApp, Files or Drive from the share sheet.
